# YouTube Shorts Optimization & Production Package

YouTube Shorts succeed or fail on two core metrics: **Viewed vs Swiped Away %** (target: >75%) and **Average Percentage Viewed (APV)** (target: >100% via seamless loops). This skill engineers high-velocity vertical video production packages with visual scene changes every 3 seconds, front-loaded hooks (0-3s), and bridges linking viewers directly to long-form videos.

## Inputs Required

| Input | Required | Description |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Channel Niche** | Yes | Content category (e.g. coding tips, fitness workouts, cooking) |
| **Content Concept / Long-Form Source** | Yes | Standalone idea or long-form video URL/transcript to clip |
| **Shorts Primary Goal** | Yes | `growth` (viral reach) \| `monetisation` (RPM revenue) \| `long-form funnel` (driving long-form views) |
| **Target Audience** | No | Demographics or viewer persona |

## Step-by-Step Procedure

1. **Format & Duration Selection**:
   - **13-18 Second Sweet Spot**: For punchy visual reveals, pattern interrupts, and instant loops (>120% APV target).
   - **45-58 Second Sweet Spot**: For step-by-step tutorials, breakdowns, or micro-stories (>85% APV target).
   - *Avoid the 30-40s dead zone* unless content density strictly requires it.
2. **First-Frame Hook Design (0:00 - 0:02)**:
   - First frame must feature immediate visual movement or on-screen tension to prevent swipe-away.
   - Text overlay must be centered within the 9:16 safe zone (avoiding top title and right-hand UI action buttons).
3. **Pacing & Visual Change Markers**:
   - Mandate a visual change, camera cut, B-roll insert, or graphic transition every 2 to 3 seconds minimum.
4. **Seamless Loop Engineering**:
   - The final sentence or visual frame must grammatically and visually bridge back into the opening sentence, causing viewers to watch a second time before realizing the video restarted.
5. **SEO & Related Video Bridge**:
   - Title: 4-6 words (20-40 characters), punchy and curiosity-driven.
   - Related Video Link: Tag the specific long-form video timestamp this Short relates to.

## Quality Constraints
- Always mandate visual cuts or graphic transitions at least once every 3 seconds.
- Keep on-screen text strictly within the 9:16 vertical safe margins (middle 60% of screen).
