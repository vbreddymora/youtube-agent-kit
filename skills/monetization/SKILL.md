# YouTube Monetisation Strategy & 7 Revenue Streams

Relying exclusively on AdSense is the most common financial vulnerability for YouTube creators. This skill conducts a comprehensive audit across all 7 YouTube revenue streams, calculates niche-specific RPM projections, builds a brand deal sponsorship rate card, and outlines an external conversion funnel to maximize creator income.

## Inputs Required

| Input | Required | Description |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Subscriber Count** | Yes | Current channel size |
| **Monthly Views** | Yes | Approximate monthly views across long-form and Shorts |
| **Channel Niche** | Yes | Content category (finance, tech, education, gaming, vlog) |
| **Current Active Streams** | Yes | Which monetisation methods are active right now |
| **Existing Products / Offers**| No | Courses, merchandise, consulting, affiliate programs |

## The 7 Revenue Streams

1. **AdSense (Long-form Display & Video Ads)**
2. **Shorts Ad Revenue Share** ($0.04 - $0.08 RPM baseline)
3. **Channel Memberships** (Tiered recurring subscriptions)
4. **Super Chat & Super Thanks** (Direct fan donations on livestreams/videos)
5. **YouTube Shopping & Affiliate Links** (Native product shelves & affiliate tags)
6. **Brand Deals & Integrated Sponsorships** (CPM-equivalent flat-fee integrations)
7. **External Funnels** (Digital products, software, private communities, high-ticket consulting)

## Step-by-Step Procedure

1. **Assess YPP Tier Status**:
   - **Tier 0 (Pre-monetised)**: Focus on affiliate links and lead capture.
   - **Tier 1 (Expanded YPP - 500 subs + 3K watch hrs)**: Unlocks Fan Funding (Memberships, Super Thanks, Shopping).
   - **Tier 2 (Full YPP - 1K subs + 4K watch hrs)**: Unlocks AdSense and Shorts rev share.
2. **Calculate AdSense Baseline**:
   - Monthly Views × Niche RPM ÷ 1,000.
3. **Audit Revenue Gaps Across All 7 Streams**:
   - Compare current earnings against potential earnings at the creator's scale.
4. **Calculate Brand Deal Sponsorship Rate Card**:
   - Dedicated Video = `Average 30-Day Views × Sponsored CPM ($25-$60) ÷ 1000`.
   - 60-Second Integration = `Dedicated Rate × 0.50`.
5. **Design External Funnel**:
   - YouTube Video → Lead Magnet CTA → Email Sequence → Core Digital Offer.
6. **Deliver 90-Day Revenue Roadmap**.
