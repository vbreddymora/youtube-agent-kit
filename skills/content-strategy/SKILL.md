# YouTube Channel Content Strategy & 90-Day Roadmap

This skill defines the structural foundation of a YouTube channel. It designs a distinctive positioning statement using the micro-niche-then-expand model, constructs a 3-5 pillar content framework (Hub / Hero / Help), evaluates niche CPM and competition viability, recommends upload cadences based on creator resources, and sets concrete 30/60/90-day milestones.

## Inputs Required

| Input | Required | Description |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Creator Background** | Yes | Skills, expertise, on-camera comfort, distinct perspective |
| **Target Audience** | Yes | Specific ideal viewer persona and pain points |
| **Niche / Category** | Yes | Initial micro-niche (e.g., solo indie game dev devlogs) |
| **Current Channel State** | Yes | Subscriber count, video count, monthly views, upload history |
| **Resource Level** | No | Solo creator, small team (2-3), or full production studio (default: solo) |
| **Primary Goal** | No | Rapid Growth / High-Ticket Monetisation / Authority / Community |

## Step-by-Step Procedure

1. **Select Channel Archetype**:
   - Classify the channel into one of the 9 templates in `templates/` to inherit niche benchmark expectations.
2. **Draft the One-Sentence Positioning Statement**:
   - Formula: `[Channel] helps [Target Viewer] achieve [Desirable Transformation] through [Unique Angle/Format] without [Major Common Friction].`
   - Map the micro-niche starting point and define the expansion milestones (at 10K, 50K, 100K subs).
3. **Build the Hub / Hero / Help Pillar Framework**:
   - **Help Content (Search / Evergreen)**: 40-50% of uploads. Solves urgent questions, generates continuous passive search traffic.
   - **Hub Content (Community / Retention)**: 30-40% of uploads. Recurring formats, personality-driven, deepens subscriber loyalty.
   - **Hero Content (Tentpole / Viral Reach)**: 10-20% of uploads. High-production, broad-appeal, designed for browse/viral push.
4. **Evaluate Niche Viability**:
   - Assess estimated CPM/RPM range, advertiser density, and content shelf-life (evergreen vs trend).
5. **Determine Upload Cadence**:
   - Solo creator: 1 high-quality long-form/week + 2 Shorts.
   - Small team: 2 long-form/week + 3 Shorts.
   - Prioritize production sustainability over burn-out frequencies.
6. **Construct 30 / 60 / 90-Day Milestones**.

## Constraints
- Never advise unfeasible upload volumes that lead to creator burnout.
- Every content pillar must map to a specific discovery mechanism (Search vs Browse vs Community).
