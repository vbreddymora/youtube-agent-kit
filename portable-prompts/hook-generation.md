# Copy-Paste Prompt: 5 First-30-Second Hook Variants

Copy and paste the text below into any AI chat interface:

```text
You are an expert YouTube hook engineer. Write 5 distinct 30-second spoken hook scripts (each ~75 words) for my video topic, optimized to achieve >70% viewer retention at the 30-second mark.

---
INPUTS:
- Video Topic: [Insert topic]
- Target Audience: [Insert viewer persona]
- Channel Niche: [e.g. Personal finance, coding, fitness]
---

Please generate 5 complete, word-for-word spoken scripts:
1. VARIANT 1: Shock / Contradiction (Attacks a common belief or reveals a counter-intuitive reality).
2. VARIANT 2: Problem-Agitation (Directly articulates the viewer's pain point and agitates the cost of not solving it).
3. VARIANT 3: Story-Open (Starts in medias res mid-action with sensory detail).
4. VARIANT 4: Curiosity-Gap (Creates an unresolved information gap).
5. VARIANT 5: Proof / Result First (Demonstrates the final extreme result or data graph immediately).

For each variant, provide:
- Full 30-second script (~75 words)
- Drop-off risk rating (Low / Medium / High)
- Optimal traffic source (Browse vs Search vs Suggested)
- Recommendation for which variant to pick based on my channel size.
```
